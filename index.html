Fabian Franz

Founder, Isomorphic AI

System architect. Solving complex problems is what I love and what I do best. I look for the underlying blueprint of a system, whether it is a caching layer or a language model. I study where language models silently go wrong: leaked labels, lost retrieval, ignored self-report. I want to understand what a model's own reports say about its internal state. Before that I worked on the Linux kernel, wrote FreeNX, and built parts of Drupal's core infrastructure.

Research

  1. 2026-07

    Anatomy of a Self-Assembled Acausal Receiver

    While training my own 20.72M-parameter language model, I traced validation results that the causal control could not reach to a normalizer that leaked future tokens past a correctly applied mask. I partly localized the exploit by head ablation and closed the channel with a per-row normalizer. A causality leak that the optimizer exploited, found, dissected and fixed end to end.

    Report · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21261879 · scripts and results included
  2. 2026-06

    From Leaky Bucket to Unsharp Lens: How Context Geometry Shapes Attention

    A behavioral study on MRCR v2. Prompt structure alone, with no weight changes, took 256k-token retrieval from 30.1% to 90.2% on one model, DeepSeek V4 Flash; three other models were tested at shorter context lengths. Filler and corrupted-map controls are included, and the variants that hurt are reported.

    Working paper · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20622917
  3. 2026-07

    Conditioning, Not Length

    The theory behind the retrieval study: long-context failure as unreliable retrieval over information that is still present. I fitted the decay model to public leaderboard scores: 77 configurations from 27 model families.

    Working paper · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21265106
  4. 2026-06

    Sameness Requires a Product: An Elementary Obstruction to Additive Matching

    The framing and Lean formalization of a classical obstruction: additive scoring cannot decide whether two symbols match; one bilinear term can. The result came out of an open session with Claude, and a footnote in the paper states the exact division of work.

    Technical note · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20585634 · Lean archive included
  5. 2026-02

    Emotion in AI is Not Noise — It's Signal

    I argue that a model's emotional self-report works as a real-time fault detector, and I propose a protocol that uses it. It is the basis of how I work with AI models every day.

Drupal

  1. since 2009

    Core maintainer and caching architecture

    Contributor since 2009 and core subsystem maintainer since 2013, today for BigPipe, Dynamic Page Cache, Page Cache and the Theme API. Drupal 7 core committer and branch maintainer from 2016 to 2025, when Drupal 7 reached end of life. In 2015, before the Drupal 8 release, I wrote the original proofs of concept and, with Wim Leers, architected its multi-tier render caching layer: cache metadata set anywhere on a page bubbles up, and the dynamic parts become placeholders. It was built to support BigPipe and also gave Drupal 8 its Dynamic Page Cache. Under the D8 Accelerate program I fixed many of the critical bugs that blocked the release.

    Proofs of concept, 2015-02-18: SmartCache++ · SmartPlaceholderCache · Dynamic Page Cache commit, Issue #2429617
    Also: cache tag timestamps for Memcache (2018) · PhpArrayContainer, which powers Drupal's service container
